Before you begin

Everything about your application, in one place.

This guide follows the five registration steps and explains what the form asks for. For binding competition rules, read the Kingdom Content & Performance Policy, Contestants’ Handbook and Competition Terms & Conditions.

Getting started

Who can register?

Applicants must be at least 6 years old on 4 December 2026. The form assigns the age group using age on that date: Kids (6–12), Teens (13–17), or Open (18+). Applicants under 18 need parent or legal guardian authorisation.

When is age calculated?

Age is calculated as of 4 December 2026, the stated finale date. Enter the applicant’s actual date of birth; the age group is then calculated automatically.

Is registration free? How long does it take?

Yes. The form describes registration as free and estimates about five minutes. Have your contact/location information, act details and YouTube audition link ready before starting.

Can I register a group?

Yes. Select Group in the participation type field. Enter a representative’s personal details in Step 1, then provide the group name, number of performers, and each performer’s name and age in Step 2. The form allows 2–100 performers.

What if my group includes performers from different age groups?

Answer Yes to the mixed-age question and describe the age mix when prompted. The form says organisers will make a classification decision; consult the Competition Terms & Conditions for the applicable rules.

Do all group members need to agree and provide permissions?

Yes. The form states that each performer must give their own declarations. Every group member under 18 needs verified parent or guardian authorisation. The festival team will contact the representative about individual permissions required.

Step 1 · Personal details

Whose details should I enter for a group?

Enter the group representative’s details. The representative should use a working email and phone/WhatsApp number and choose how they prefer to be contacted.

Why do you ask for my date of birth and location?

Date of birth determines eligibility, age group and whether guardian authorisation is required. Country, state/region and city/town identify where you are joining from and help the team assess participation arrangements.

What contact details should I provide?

Provide an email address and phone or WhatsApp number, including the country code. Then select Email, Phone call or WhatsApp/messaging as your preferred contact method.

Can someone under 18 apply?

Yes, applicants aged 6–17 may apply, but a parent or legal guardian must provide authorisation. The guardian’s full name, relationship, email and phone are requested in the final step. The festival team will verify authorisation directly before a minor is shortlisted.

What if the form says I’m not eligible?

The form’s minimum age is 6 on 4 December 2026. Check that the date of birth is entered correctly. If it is correct and the applicant will be under 6 on that date, the form says they are not eligible.

Step 2 · Talent and act

Which categories are available?

Choose one of Music, Dance, Public Speaking & Spoken Word, or Drama & Creative Skits. The performance type options depend on the category selected. See the festival disciplines and prizes.

What performance types can I select?

Music: singing, instrumental performance, or approved musical act. Dance: solo or group dance performance. Public Speaking & Spoken Word: poetry, storytelling, oratory, or spoken performance. Drama & Creative Skits: creative skit, original skit, comedy, or theatrical performance.

What should my act description include?

Briefly explain what you will perform, including instruments or stage requirements. The field allows up to 1,200 characters. Your performance must explicitly communicate a biblical truth, Christ-centred message, or recognisable aspect of the Christian faith; merely wholesome or inspirational content without an identifiable Christian message does not meet the stated requirement. Read the full policy.

Must my performance be original?

The form asks whether it is original (Yes, No or Partly), but the audition and performance declaration requires you to own or have the necessary permissions for material you submit and perform, including music, lyrics, recordings, choreography and scripts. See the Terms & Conditions for complete rights requirements.

What does the finale participation question mean?

The stated finale is Friday, 4 December 2026, in Port Harcourt. Select whether you can attend in person if selected, are applying internationally and need arrangements explained, or need to discuss arrangements. Do not book travel until the festival team confirms arrangements.

Can I note accessibility, equipment or other needs?

Yes. Use the optional Special requirements field for accessibility, equipment, backing track or similar needs. The form does not guarantee that a request can be accommodated, so raise time-sensitive questions with the festival team.

Step 3 · Audition

How long should my audition video be?

It must be between 60 and 120 seconds and feature the actual contestant or group. You must confirm this in the form.

How do I submit the audition?

Upload the video to YouTube, title it “Dominion Festival 3.0 | Your Name | Category,” set it to Unlisted or Public (not Private), then paste its share link into the form. File uploads are not accepted. Accepted links include youtube.com/watch, youtu.be and youtube.com/shorts. More tips are in the Contestants’ Handbook.

Can the video be Private, or can I upload a file instead?

No. The form requires an Unlisted or Public YouTube link and explicitly says Private videos and file uploads are not accepted. Unlisted videos can be viewed by people with the link.

Does the audition need to be the same act I described?

The form asks for the category, performance type, act title and description, then requires a video demonstrating the applicant’s talent. Make the video clearly represent the act you are applying with. Consult the Handbook for audition and performance guidance.

Do I need permission from people shown in the video?

Yes. You must confirm you have permission to submit the video and that everyone shown has agreed to its use for application review. You also confirm that you have permission for the material performed.

What if my YouTube link is rejected?

Use the video’s share link and check that it is a supported YouTube format (watch, youtu.be or shorts), has no access restriction beyond Unlisted/Public, and opens correctly. The form does not accept links from other video hosts.

Step 4 · Review

Can I check my answers before submitting?

Yes. Step 4 displays a summary of key details, including contact information, location, category, participation type, act, language, video link and finale choice. Use Back to return to earlier steps and correct anything before proceeding.

What should I do if I spot an error?

Use the Back button to return to the relevant step, update the answer and continue through the review again. Check spelling, contact details, group member information and that the video link works.

Step 5 · Declarations and consent

Why are there so many declarations?

They record your agreement to accuracy and eligibility, the Kingdom Content policy, competition terms, handbook, conduct and integrity, performance rights, privacy acknowledgement, and audition/event recording terms. Read each statement and its linked policy before accepting.

Which declarations are required?

All applicable mandatory declarations must be accepted to submit. The form does not pre-tick them. For applicants under 18, guardian details and guardian consent are also required. The two additional permissions for publicity and future communications are optional.

What is the difference between required and optional media consent?

The required recording declaration acknowledges that auditions and performances may be recorded and used for judging, assessment, event production and competition records, as explained in the published competition documents. Separate optional publicity consent authorises selected photos, interviews, excerpts and recordings for promotion. The form says declining optional permissions will not affect eligibility or judging.

Do I have to agree to future emails or opportunities?

No. Future Opportunities & Communications is optional. Leaving it unchecked does not affect competition eligibility or judging.

Where can I read the Privacy Notice?

The registration form says the Privacy Notice link is to be published on the official website. It asks applicants to acknowledge receipt of the notice, which explains processing of registration information, audition materials and related records. Contact the festival team if you cannot locate it before submitting.

After you submit

How do I know my application was received?

On successful submission, the page displays “Application received,” a message and a reference code. Save the reference. The form says this confirms registration only; it is not a selection notice.

Does submitting mean I have been selected?

No. Submission confirms the application was received. The festival team will review it and contact you using your preferred contact method. Applicants under 18 are not shortlisted until guardian authorisation has been verified.

When will I hear back?

The registration form does not state a review or response timeline. Keep access to the email address and phone number you provided, and contact the festival team if you need an update.

What if I get an error or cannot submit?

Check that all required fields and declarations are complete, the YouTube link is valid, and the connection is working. If the problem continues, note the error and contact the festival team at info@rccgdc.org or +234 908 748 4840.

How can I ask a question not covered here?

Contact the festival team at info@rccgdc.org or +234 908 748 4840. You can also use the site’s Contact page.
