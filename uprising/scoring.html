How You Are Scored

A transparent scorecard for every act

Demo scoring rules. This scorecard is a working draft to show how judging will be transparent. The weights and criteria may be modified by the festival team before judging begins, and any change will be published on this page.

The scorecard

Each judge scores every act out of 100 using the same five criteria. The same scorecard is used for the audition round and the live finale.

Criterion What judges look for Points
Skill & Technique Mastery of the craft: accuracy, control, difficulty and consistency. 30
Creativity & Originality Fresh ideas, personal style and a distinctive interpretation. 25
Stage Presence & Delivery Confidence, energy, expression and command of the audience. 20
Impact & Message Emotional connection, memorability and how well the act fits the ARISE spirit. 15
Presentation & Rules Staying within the time limit, appropriate content and quality of the audition video. 10
Total 100

What each discipline looks for

  • Skill: pitch, tone, rhythm and breath control for vocalists; accuracy and fluency for instrumentalists.
  • Creativity: arrangement, interpretation and originality of song choice or composition.
  • Delivery: expression, stage confidence and connection with the audience.
  • Skill: technique, timing, balance and precision. For groups, synchronisation.
  • Creativity: choreography, use of space and musicality.
  • Delivery: energy, expression and stage formation.
  • Skill: clarity, diction, pacing, vocal variety and command of language.
  • Creativity: originality of content, structure and imagery.
  • Delivery: confidence, emotional range and persuasiveness.
  • Skill: acting craft, characterisation, timing and diction.
  • Creativity: script originality, concept and staging.
  • Delivery: believability, ensemble work and audience engagement.

How winners are decided

  1. Audition round: each audition video is scored by the judging panel using the scorecard above. The top-scoring acts are shortlisted as finalists (provisionally 8 to 12 acts).
  2. Finale: finalists perform live on Friday, 4 December 2026. Each judge scores every act out of 100.
  3. Discipline winners: within each discipline, the three highest finale scores take 1st, 2nd and 3rd place.
  4. Overall Champion: the act with the highest finale score across all disciplines is crowned Overall Champion.
To be confirmed: number of judges, how scores are averaged, how ties are resolved, whether any audience or online voting contributes to the result, and whether kids, teens and open entries are ranked together or separately within a discipline.

Fairness

  • Judges score independently and declare any conflict of interest.
  • Scores are recorded on a standard scorecard and retained for review.
  • The decisions of the judges are final.
