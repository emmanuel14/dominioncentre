Draft rules. These rules are provisional and may be updated by the festival team before registration closes.

Competition disciplines

Discipline What is included
Music Singing, instrumental performances and approved musical acts.
Dance Solo and group dance performances.
Public Speaking & Spoken Word Poetry, storytelling, oratory and spoken performance.
Drama & Creative Skits Drama, original skits, comedy and theatrical performance.

Age groups

  • Kids: 6 to 12 years
  • Teens: 13 to 17 years
  • Open: 18 years and above

Age is calculated as of 4 December 2026 , the date of the finale. Applicants must be at least 6 years old on that date.

Individual and group entries

  • Individual and group entries are accepted in every discipline.
  • For a group entry, the registrant is the group representative and must list every performer's name and age.
  • Each performer may only appear in the number of entries the festival permits (to be confirmed).

Mixed-age groups

To be confirmed by the festival team. Mixed-age group eligibility rules (for example, which age group a mixed group competes in) will be published here before mixed-age group applications are accepted.

Audition video: how to submit

Video length: your audition must be between 60 and 120 seconds . Format: a YouTube link is required. File uploads are not accepted.

Recording tips

  • The video must feature the actual contestant or the full group that will perform.
  • Make sure the performer's face and full performance are clearly visible and the sound is clear.
  • Film horizontally (landscape) if you can, in good light, with minimal background noise.
  • Show your real talent. Do not use pre-recorded studio tracks of your own voice, edited clips or heavy filters that hide your ability.

Upload to YouTube in 6 steps

  1. Open youtube.com (or the YouTube app) and sign in with a Google account. Parents or guardians can upload for children.
  2. Tap or click Create , then Upload video , and select your audition video.
  3. Title it: Dominion Festival 3.0 | Your Name or Group Name | Discipline .
  4. When asked "Is this video made for kids?", choose the option that applies to your video.
  5. Set Visibility to Unlisted (recommended) or Public . Do not choose Private, because judges will not be able to open it.
  6. After uploading, tap Share , copy the link, and paste it into the "YouTube video link" field on the registration form .

Accepted link types: youtube.com/watch?v=… , youtu.be/… , youtube.com/shorts/… . Videos shorter than 60 seconds or longer than 120 seconds may be disqualified.

Free registration

Registration is free for every eligible applicant. No one will ask you for payment to register or to be shortlisted.

International applications

Applicants from any country may submit a video application. Selected finalists will be contacted by the festival team to confirm finale participation arrangements.

Please do not book travel until the festival team has confirmed arrangements with you in writing.
To be confirmed: how finalists based abroad can take part in the live finale (in person, or by another arrangement) will be published here.

Applicants under 18

  • A parent or legal guardian must give permission as part of registration.
  • For group entries, permission is required for every minor performer.
  • The festival will apply appropriate child-protection arrangements to all minors' participation. See the safeguarding section .

Finalists

We are provisionally planning for 8 to 12 finalist acts . The final number depends on the quality of entries, judging outcomes and available programme time. Meeting the minimum requirements does not guarantee selection.

Conduct

  • All performances must be suitable for a church family audience.
  • Entries must not infringe anyone else's copyright. See the terms .
  • The decisions of the judges are final.
