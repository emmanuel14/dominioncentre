About ARISE

Your gift deserves a stage

Dominion Festival is the talent platform of RCCG Dominion Centre, raising Kingdom stars of today and the future. Dominion Festival 3.0 | ARISE invites kids, teens and adults, locally and from around the world, to submit an audition video, be shortlisted and perform before a live audience in Port Harcourt.

1

Register for free

Complete the simple, mobile-friendly form on the registration page .

2

Submit a YouTube audition

Record a clear 60 to 120 second video of your act and share the YouTube link. See the step-by-step guide .

3

Judged and shortlisted

Judges score every audition using the published scoring system . We plan for 8 to 12 finalist acts.

4

The grand finale

Finalists perform live on Friday, 4 December 2026 in Port Harcourt. Winners are crowned in every discipline, and one Overall Champion rises.

Questions

Quick answers

Does it cost anything to register?

No. Registration is completely free.

How long should my audition video be?

Between 60 and 120 seconds, uploaded to YouTube. Full instructions are on the rules page .

Can I enter as a group?

Yes. Individual and group entries are accepted in every discipline. Mixed-age group rules are published on the rules page.

I am under 18. Can I take part?

Yes. Kids (6–12) and Teens (13–17) have their own age groups. A parent or legal guardian must give permission during registration.

How many acts reach the finale?

We are planning provisionally for 8 to 12 finalist acts, subject to quality, judging and available programme time.

How are performances scored?

Judges use a transparent, published scorecard. See the scoring page .
