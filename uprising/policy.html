UPRISING 3.0 — Kingdom Content & Performance Policy

Open to all talents. Dedicated to God’s Kingdom.

UPRISING 3.0 is a Christian-founded talent discovery and development platform, established to identify, nurture and elevate creative gifts for the glory of God and the advancement of His Kingdom. The competition welcomes talented individuals and groups from all backgrounds, irrespective of their religious affiliation, denomination or established artistic genre.

The content requirement

Every song, dance, spoken-word presentation, dramatic performance or other creative expression submitted for UPRISING 3.0 must explicitly communicate a biblical truth, a Christ-centered message or a recognizable aspect of the Christian faith. This requirement applies equally to all contestants, whether they ordinarily perform gospel or secular content.

General inspirational, motivational, romantic, cultural or socially positive content, without an explicit biblical or Christ-centered message, will not qualify merely because it is wholesome or morally acceptable.

Creative freedom

Contestants are free to explore different creative styles, musical genres and artistic expressions, provided that their performances clearly communicate the Christian message and uphold the dignity, holiness and values of God’s Kingdom.

Content standards

All performances must also be free from profanity, sexually explicit or obscene material, degrading language, glorification of criminality or substance abuse, and other content inconsistent with the Christian foundation of UPRISING.

Screening and enforcement

The organizers reserve the right to screen proposed content, request appropriate modifications, reject non-compliant entries or disqualify contestants who materially violate these requirements, in accordance with the published competition rules and review procedures.

Acceptance

By entering UPRISING 3.0, every contestant expressly acknowledges and accepts this Kingdom Content & Performance Policy as a fundamental condition of participation.
