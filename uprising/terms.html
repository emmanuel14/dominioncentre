1. About the competition

Dominion Festival 3.0 | ARISE is a free talent competition organised by RCCG Dominion Centre, #199 Woji Road, GRA Phase 2, Port Harcourt. The finale is planned for Friday, 4 December 2026 in Port Harcourt, Nigeria.

2. Eligibility

Entry is open to individuals and groups in the Kids (6–12), Teens (13–17) and Open (18+) age groups, calculated as of 4 December 2026. See the rules page for full details.

3. Prizes

  • In each of the four competition disciplines: 1st place ₦500,000, 2nd place ₦300,000, 3rd place ₦200,000.
  • The Overall Champion receives an additional ₦1,000,000.
  • Prizes are paid in Nigerian naira. Payment method and timing: to be confirmed by the festival team .
  • Group prizes are awarded to the group as a whole. How a group prize is shared is the group's responsibility.
  • Prizes for minors will be paid to a parent or legal guardian.

4. Originality and permissions

  • You confirm that you have permission to submit the audition video and that everyone shown has agreed to its use for application review.
  • You are responsible for any music, text or other material you use. Do not submit content that infringes someone else's rights.
  • The festival may use clips and photographs of finalists and winners for promotion of the festival. Scope of use to be confirmed.

5. Disqualification

Entries may be disqualified for false information, videos that do not meet the length or format rules, inappropriate content, or breach of these terms.

6. Judging

Performances are judged using the published scorecard . The decisions of the judges are final.

7. Privacy

  • We collect the details you give on the registration form (including contact details, age group, location, performance details and your YouTube link) to administer and review your application.
  • Application data and audition links are accessible only to authorised festival staff and judges, stored securely and kept only as long as needed. Retention period to be confirmed.
  • We will not sell your information.
  • To ask about, correct or remove your data, contact info@rccgdc.org .

8. Safeguarding of minors

  • Applicants under 18 need permission from a parent or legal guardian, collected during registration.
  • The festival will apply its child-protection procedures at the finale, including supervised areas and controlled access for performers under 18. Detailed procedures to be published by the festival team.
  • Guardians should only submit videos they are comfortable being reviewed by the judging panel. Do not include personal details such as home addresses in videos.
  • Concerns can be reported to info@rccgdc.org or +234 908 748 4840.

9. International applicants

International applicants are welcome to apply by video. Finale participation arrangements will be confirmed with selected applicants. The festival is not responsible for travel costs incurred before written confirmation.

10. Changes

The organisers may update these terms, the rules and the scoring system. Changes will be published on this website.
