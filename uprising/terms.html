UPRISING 3.0 • Friday, 4 December 2026 • Port Harcourt, Nigeria

1. Organiser, event and status

UPRISING 3.0 is an emerging-talent competition within Dominion Festival 3.0 — ARISE, organised by RCCG Dominion Centre, Port Harcourt (“Organiser”). The scheduled UPRISING night is Friday 4 December 2026, from 5:00 PM until approximately 2:00 AM on Saturday 5 December. Dates, venue and programme arrangements remain subject to reasonable changes notified through official channels.

Draft. These Terms are a draft. Before launch the Organiser must publish a version number, effective date, official contact details, confirmed deadlines, venue and the final Privacy Notice.

2. Eligibility and entry

Entry is open to eligible participants irrespective of religious affiliation or nationality, subject to applicable law, event capacity and stated content standards. Age divisions, measured on 4 December 2026: Kids 6–12; Teens 13–17; Open 18+. Applicants younger than six are not eligible under the current proposal.

Applicants may enter as individuals or groups. Group members must be identified; each adult member must agree to the rules, and every minor member requires verified parent/guardian authorisation. Mixed-age groups are reviewed individually for suitable placement and safeguarding; the Organiser will publish its classification decision before competition.

Registration is free. One person should not submit duplicate or misleading applications to gain an unfair advantage.

To be confirmed: entry limits per person/category and international remote-participation eligibility.

3. Categories and audition requirements

Categories: Music; Dance; Public Speaking & Spoken Word; Drama & Creative Skits. Subcategories and time limits will be announced on the registration page.

Applicants must submit a genuine 60–120-second audition video (proposed standard), by approved upload or accessible unlisted link, with an accurate description of the performance. The Organiser may request proof of identity, age, authorship, guardian authority or additional audition material.

No impersonation, AI-generated substitution presented as an authentic human performance, manipulated scoring, purchased votes, bots, multiple identities or unauthorised access to the registration or judging systems. Permitted production assistance and AI usage rules should be published explicitly before auditions open.

4. Content and conduct standards

UPRISING is not limited to gospel performances, but all material must be wholesome and suitable for a broad family audience in keeping with the Organiser’s Christian foundation. Prohibited content includes unlawful material; credible threats; hateful or discriminatory abuse; explicit sexual content; dangerous stunts without approved controls; harassment; and material that exploits or endangers children.

Participants must respect organisers, other contestants, judges, volunteers and audiences. Bribery, intimidation, bullying, retaliation, deliberate disruption and attempts to influence judging improperly are prohibited.

The Organiser may request edits or an alternative performance before excluding an act, where safe and practical. Any restriction will be applied consistently and without unlawful discrimination.

See also the Kingdom Content & Performance Policy , which sets out the content requirement every entry must meet.

5. Original work and third-party rights

Contestants retain ownership of their original performances and underlying creative works. Submission does not transfer copyright or create an exclusive management agreement.

Contestants warrant that they have necessary permissions for third-party compositions, backing tracks, scripts, images, choreography, samples and recordings. An applicant must not assume that attribution alone grants permission. Venue, broadcast and online music clearances may also be needed from the relevant rightsholders or licensing bodies.

Subject to the separate media choice and lawful bases described in the Privacy Notice, contestants grant only the limited, non-exclusive permissions necessary to assess submissions and administer the competition. Wider promotional exploitation requires the relevant authorisation or other valid legal basis.

6. Judging and selection

A qualified panel will evaluate contestants against a published rubric. Proposed weighting: Technical Skill 35%; Creativity/Originality 25%; Performance & Delivery 20%; Audience Connection 10%; Overall Impact 10%. The Organiser may adapt rubrics by category and publish the final criteria before scoring begins.

Judges must disclose conflicts of interest and recuse themselves where appropriate. Scoring access must be restricted and logged. The Organiser may investigate irregularities, correct clerical errors and review evidence of bias or misconduct.

Selection decisions will be made in good faith based on published criteria and available capacity. Contestants may request an administrative review of procedural errors through uprising@rccgdominioncentre.org within 72 hours; a review is not a promise to overturn artistic judgment.

7. Prizes, costs and travel

Prize descriptions, eligibility, payment timing, taxes, conditions and any non-cash benefits must be published before contestants are asked to rely on them. No prize amount is promised by these draft Terms.

Unless expressly agreed in writing, participants remain responsible for transport, travel documentation, visas, accommodation, meals and personal costs. The Organiser does not guarantee visas, sponsorship, travel support, professional contracts or future bookings. International applicants should not incur travel expenses before receiving finalist confirmation and the event’s travel instructions.

8. Safeguarding and health and safety

The Organiser will appoint a safeguarding lead and provide an accessible reporting route before registration opens. Minors require verified guardian consent; no judge, staff member or volunteer should privately contact or meet a child outside approved safeguarded arrangements. Child performances, rehearsals, dressing areas, transport and backstage access must be age-appropriate and supervised.

Dangerous props, pyrotechnics, aerial acts, weapons, hazardous materials and physically risky performances require advance written clearance and may be prohibited. Participants must disclose relevant technical risks without being required to disclose unrelated sensitive health details. Reasonable accessibility accommodations may be requested confidentially.

The Organiser may pause or stop a performance where there is an immediate safety concern. Emergency procedures, venue capacity and appropriate first-aid arrangements must be confirmed before the event.

9. Privacy, security and communications

Personal data will be processed in accordance with the published Privacy Notice and applicable Nigerian data-protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 where applicable. Collect only information reasonably necessary, restrict access by role, protect uploads and guardian records, define retention periods and provide a data-rights contact.

Contestants should use only the official registration site linked from rccgdominioncentre.org and official announced channels. Organisers will not request passwords, OTPs or unofficial “selection fees.” Suspected fraud or security incidents should be reported promptly.

Contestant contact information must not be shared with sponsors, judges or other contestants for unrelated purposes. Optional marketing requires a separate choice and unsubscribe route.

10. Disqualification and fair process

Grounds may include materially false information, ineligibility, fraud, safeguarding risks, serious misconduct, rights infringement or repeated refusal to follow reasonable safety directions.

Where feasible, the contestant will be informed of the concern and given an opportunity to respond before a final adverse decision; urgent safeguarding or security cases may require immediate interim suspension. Decisions and reasons should be documented and accessible to authorised administrators only.

11. Changes, cancellation and responsibility

The Organiser may make proportionate changes for safety, security, legal requirements, force majeure, venue constraints or other genuine operational reasons, with prompt notice through official channels. If the event is cancelled or significantly altered, participants will be informed of available options.

Nothing in these Terms excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, limits statutory consumer or data-protection rights, or requires a contestant to waive rights arising from negligence or misconduct where such waiver would be unlawful. Contestants are responsible for losses caused by their own proven unlawful or wilful acts to the extent permitted by law; no unlimited blanket indemnity is imposed.

12. Complaints, governing law and acceptance

Concerns should first be submitted to the published Organiser contact. Safeguarding complaints must have an independent escalation route. Unresolved disputes are subject to applicable Nigerian law and competent courts, without removing any mandatory rights available to international contestants.

By ticking the required agreement boxes and submitting the application, an adult applicant confirms acceptance of the final published Terms. For a minor, a verified parent/guardian must accept on the child’s behalf. The application must preserve the exact Terms version accepted.
