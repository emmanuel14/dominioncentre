Welcome to UPRISING 3.0

Your gift deserves a stage. The world deserves to see it.

UPRISING 3.0 is the third edition of UPRISING and the first intentionally designed as a talent hunt. It is part of Dominion Festival 3.0 — ARISE, whose scriptural inspiration is Isaiah 60:1: “Arise, shine; for thy light is come, and the glory of the LORD is risen upon thee.”

We are looking for emerging talent, creativity, excellence and positive influence. Applicants of different backgrounds are welcome. You do not have to perform gospel-only material; performances must, however, meet our wholesome family audience standards.

At a glance

  • Festival: Wednesday 2 to Friday 4 December 2026; 5:00 PM daily.
  • Day 1: In-Reach & Special Services (to 8:30 PM).
  • Day 2: Family Celebrations & Special Programmes (to 8:30 PM).
  • Day 3: UPRISING Night (Friday 5:00 PM to Saturday 5 December, 2:00 AM).
  • Dominion Festival Thanksgiving: Sunday 6 December 2026 (separate from the three-day evening festival programme).
  • Venue: RCCG Dominion Centre, 199 Woji Road, GRA Phase 2, Port Harcourt, Nigeria.

Who can apply?

Age divisions on 4 December 2026: Kids 6–12 ; Teens 13–17 ; Open 18+ . Individual and group applications are welcome, subject to the published rules. Every participant under 18 must have verified parent/guardian consent.

Talent categories: Music; Dance; Public Speaking & Spoken Word; Drama & Creative Skits. Mixed-age groups will receive a classification decision from the organisers.

Your journey from application to stage

1

Apply

Complete the official online form through the UPRISING page linked from rccgdominioncentre.org. Registration is free; keep your reference number.

2

Submit audition

Upload or link a clear 60–120-second video showing your actual ability. Follow published format and access instructions.

3

Screening

Administrators check eligibility, completeness, permissions and content suitability.

4

Judging and callbacks

Shortlisted acts may be asked for further material or a live/virtual callback. Dates and selection criteria will be communicated through official channels.

5

Finalist confirmation

Selected acts receive reporting details, performance time limits, technical requirements and rehearsal instructions. Do not make non-refundable travel arrangements until confirmed.

6

UPRISING Night

Finalists participate in the showcase and competition, with results and prizes announced according to the final programme.

How to prepare a strong audition

Record in a quiet, well-lit place where your performance is easy to see and hear. Introduce your act briefly, then demonstrate your strongest material. Avoid unnecessary edits that misrepresent your ability.

Choose family-appropriate content. If you perform a cover, adaptation or copyrighted piece, identify it accurately and secure required permissions. Do not use another person’s video as your own.

For groups, show the actual participating members and specify the group leader. Keep the audition link accessible to the authorised team until the review process ends.

How judging works

The proposed scoring model considers the following weighting. The final category-specific rubric will be published before judging.

Criterion Weight
Technical Skill 35%
Creativity / Originality 25%
Performance & Delivery 20%
Audience Connection 10%
Overall Impact 10%

Selection is competitive and spaces are limited. Constructive feedback may be offered where resources permit, but individual feedback is not guaranteed. Artistic decisions are distinct from administrative errors; report suspected conflicts, unfair conduct or scoring irregularities through the official complaints route.

What finalists should expect

Finalists must follow the approved performance duration, sound-check and rehearsal schedule. Declare microphones, instruments, backing tracks, projection, stage props, lighting requests and any accessibility needs in advance.

No last-minute substitution of members, music or routines without permission. Arrive at the confirmed check-in time with approved identification and, for minors, the required guardian or approved chaperone.

The UPRISING night is a vigil ending around 2:00 AM. Parents/guardians must agree appropriate child participation, rest, supervision and safe departure arrangements. Younger contestants may have earlier performance or departure arrangements determined by safeguarding requirements.

Respect, safety and safeguarding

We expect respect for other contestants, judges, crew, guests and audiences. Bullying, discrimination, harassment, bribery, vote manipulation, unsafe acts and backstage access breaches are not tolerated.

Never send passwords or one-time codes to anyone claiming to represent UPRISING. Registration is free. Report suspicious payment demands or messages through the official channel.

If you are under 18, keep your parent/guardian involved in all communications. You should never be asked to meet a judge or team member privately. Tell your guardian and the safeguarding lead immediately if anything makes you uncomfortable.

Safeguarding and emergency contacts on event night: Jeff Ibim – 07062300925 and Johnson Stanley – 08064656067 .

Your creative work and your privacy

Your original creative work remains yours. We need limited permission to assess auditions and manage the event; optional broader promotional uses are addressed separately in the consent form.

We collect personal details for registration, eligibility, safeguarding, communications and event administration. We do not sell contestant information. Read the Privacy Notice for retention, data rights and contact details.

Publicity recording and future marketing choices must be explained separately, especially for children.

Prizes, expenses and opportunities

UPRISING aims to discover, develop and elevate emerging talent. Prizes, mentorship and follow-on opportunities will be announced only after approval. Do not rely on unconfirmed prize claims.

Unless the Organiser explicitly offers support in writing, travel, accommodation, meals, visas and other personal expenses are your responsibility.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to belong to RCCG or be Christian?

No. The talent hunt welcomes applicants of different backgrounds who respect its content and conduct standards.

Is registration free?

Yes, under the current event plan. Report anyone asking for unofficial selection or audition fees.

Can I enter from outside Nigeria?

Applications may be received internationally; whether a finalist can participate remotely or must attend in person will be specified in the final rules.

Can a child enter?

Yes, ages 6–17 in the relevant divisions, with verified guardian consent and safeguarding requirements.

Can my group enter?

Yes, with a complete roster and individual or guardian consents as applicable.

What if I am not shortlisted?

Thank you for participating. Not being selected does not diminish your gift; watch official channels for future opportunities.

Final checklist

  • ☐ I have read the current Terms & Conditions and Privacy Notice.
  • ☐ My details and age category are accurate.
  • ☐ My audition shows my actual talent and follows the published instructions.
  • ☐ I have rights/permissions for my performance materials.
  • ☐ My guardian has completed the required consent if I am under 18.
  • ☐ I have kept my registration reference and official contact details.
  • ☐ I understand that final schedules, venue and prize details will be communicated officially.

A final word

UPRISING is more than a stage. It is an opportunity to discover gifts, develop excellence, build meaningful connections and encourage a generation to arise with purpose. Come prepared, come respectful, and give your best.
