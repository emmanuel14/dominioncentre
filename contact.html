Plan Your Visit

What to Expect

1

Arrival

Arrive 15–20 minutes early. Our parking team will guide you to available spaces.

2

Welcome

Our ushers will greet you, provide a bulletin, and help you find a seat.

3

Kids Check-In

Visit our Kids Church desk for secure check-in for children ages 3–12.

4

Enjoy the Service

Relax and enjoy worship and teaching. Services typically last 1.5–2 hours.

5

Connect

Visit our connect desk after service to meet pastors and learn how to get involved.

Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear?

Come as you are. Some dress formally, others prefer casual attire. Comfort is what matters most.

Is parking available?

Yes. We have ample parking and attendants to guide you on Sundays.

Programs for children?

We have a vibrant Kids Church for ages 3–12 during our 10:30 AM Sunday service.

How long are services?

Services typically last between 1.5 and 2 hours including worship and teaching.

Can I watch online?

Yes. We livestream on Facebook and YouTube. Past sermons are on our YouTube channel.

How do I become a member?

Contact us to learn about our Welcome Class and membership process with our pastoral team.

Still Have Questions?

We are always happy to help with any questions about our church, services, or ministries.
