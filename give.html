Common Questions

Giving FAQs

Why do we give?

Giving is an act of worship and gratitude — honoring God, supporting His work, and helping those in need.

Can I designate my giving?

Yes. Specify Tithe, Offering, Building Project, Outreach, or Welfare in your transfer description.

Tithes vs offerings?

A tithe is 10% of income given in obedience (Malachi 3:10). Offerings are gifts above the tithe as the Spirit leads.

Can non-members give?

Absolutely. Anyone led to support God's work through our ministry is welcome to give.
