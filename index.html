Welcome Home

The Redeemed Christian Church of God, Dominion Centre (RCCGDC) was birthed on the 9th of October, 2022 — a satellite church of The King's Palace Parish, Headquarters of Region 33.

We are an expression church committed to spiritual growth, fellowship, and transforming lives through the power of God's Word.
