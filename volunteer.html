Why Volunteer?

Looking to serve and grow in your faith? We're excited to welcome you into one of our church departments. Whether you love music, teaching, helping others, or working behind the scenes, there's a place for you here.

Volunteering at RCCG Dominion Centre is more than just serving — it's about discovering your purpose, developing your gifts, and making a lasting impact in the lives of others.

Discover and develop your God-given talents
Build meaningful relationships
Grow spiritually through service
Make a real difference in your community

"Not to be served, but to serve"

— Mark 10:45
